# Proposed amendments to the draft protocol (for the owner, before the freeze)

**Status update (2026-11-24, round 2): A1 (option a), A2, A3 (drop), A4 and A5 were approved by the owner and applied in `research/protocol.md` section 15 (decision D-010). A6 and A8 are information; A7 was not in the approved list and was not applied. The text below is kept unchanged as the historical proposal.**

**Original status: PROPOSALS ONLY (2026-11-24, M2 session).** Nothing here changes `research/protocol.md` or any `EXP-NNN/protocol.md`; the draft protocol v0.1 is untouched. The owner decides which proposals, if any, enter the protocol before freezing it (G-FREEZE).

**Evidence base.** Every number below comes from EXPLORATORY runs on exploratory seeds 9000-9019 (journal entries J-0002 to J-0008, summary `results/exploratory/M2-planning-summary.md`, generated by `scripts/m2_planning_summary.py`). None is a finding. Disclosure: the assistant has seen exploratory values of R in the 6x6, rho = 2 cell (the analogue of the primary cell on other seeds). To avoid post-hoc tuning, no proposal below changes the margin `delta`, the primary cell or the hypotheses; the owner may wish to decide A2 without reading the exploratory medians.

## A1. Solver: Frank-Wolfe as drafted would make the primary analysis fail by construction (high priority)

**Observation (exploratory).** With the drafted stopping rule (`RG <= 1e-6` or 20,000 iterations):

| Cell | SO nonconverged | UE nonconverged |
|---|---|---|
| 6x6, rho = 2 (X-TIMING-6x6, X-SPREAD-6x6-FW and the SO references of X-MIX-6x6; seeds 9000-9019) | 27 of 28 solves | 11 of 25 solves |
| 6x6, rho = 3 (5 seeds) | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| 4x4, rho = 2 (10 seeds) | 9 of 10 | 4 of 10 |
| 4x4, rho = 3 (10 seeds) | 10 of 10 | 9 of 10 |
| Braess with link e, SO, d in {5.1, 6.0, 7.7, 8.9, 9.3, 11.1} | 6 of 6 | UE: 3 of 3 for d in {8.9, 9.3, 11.1} |

Protocol section 7 excludes nonconverged runs from the primary analysis and declares it failed if more than 10 percent of primary-cell instances are nonconverged; with these rates EXP-003 would fail regardless of the underlying effect, EXP-001 could not verify SO with link e (FW reaches 498.0099 instead of 498 after 20,000 iterations), and every MIX(c > 0) run of EXP-004 inherits the SO nonconvergence. The cause is the known slow (sublinear) convergence of Frank-Wolfe near corner solutions: the residual gaps sit between about 3e-6 and 4e-5 at the cap.

**Candidate implemented (not in the draft).** `flow.assignment.gradient_projection`, a path-based gradient projection (unit step, Gauss-Seidel over OD pairs), with the same relative-gap definition and stopping rule. On the same 20 exploratory 6x6 instances it converged in every solve at both `1e-6` and `1e-8` (UE and SO), in 0.008 to 0.031 s mean per solve. R(UE) from nonconverged-or-converged FW and from GP at `1e-8` differ by at most 7.6e-5 (median -4.6e-6). It reaches the exact Braess SO value 498 and the exact UE values of the EXP-002 derivation (tests `test_braess_so_with_link_frank_wolfe_slow_known_limitation`, `test_engine_matches_exact_ue`).

**Options for the owner.**
- (a, recommended) Make GP the solver of record for UE and SO; keep FW as a differential reference reported in S1. MIX(c) then needs a GP follower solve with background flow (about 20 lines, not yet written; a test MIX(0) = UE and MIX(1) = SO would carry over).
- (b) Keep FW and relax the primary tolerance to `1e-4`, with S1 reporting `1e-6` and `1e-8` as sensitivity (S1 at `1e-8` would be almost entirely nonconverged).
- (c) Keep FW and raise the iteration cap (the gap decreases slowly; the projected runtime grows proportionally and convergence is still not assured).

## A2. Seed count: n = 50 may resolve the primary estimand only coarsely (medium priority)

**Observation (exploratory, 6x6, rho = 2, 20 seeds, GP at 1e-6 so that all runs converged).** Between-instance SD of R(UE) 0.0169; 95 percent percentile bootstrap interval of the median has width 0.0286 at n = 20, about 0.018 at n = 50 if the width scales as `1/sqrt(n)` (an approximation). That half-width (about 0.009) is of the same order as the margin `delta = 0.01`.

**Proposal.** Because GP makes a solve cost about 0.01 to 0.03 s, raise the instance count to n = 100 per grid size (seeds 1000-1099 and 2000-2099), projected interval width about 0.013, total cost still under a few minutes. Decide this on precision grounds alone; keep `delta`, the primary cell and the decision rule unchanged.

## A3. Degenerate demand level rho = 0.5 (medium priority)

**Observation (exploratory).** At rho = 0.5 with the FF calibration, FF loading is already the user equilibrium (R(FF) = R(UE)) in 5 of 5 exploratory 6x6 instances and 9 of 10 4x4 instances; the largest R(UE) is 1.4e-4 (6x6) and 5.0e-4 (4x4). At rho = 1, FF equals UE in 3 of 5 (6x6) and 3 of 10 (4x4) instances. The 3x3, rho = 1 smoke cell is similarly degenerate (UE in 0 iterations for all 5 instances). These levels test little beyond "no congestion, no gap".

**Proposal.** Keep rho = 0.5 as a declared descriptive control (expected R near 0) or replace it with rho = 1.5. Either way, state in the protocol that rho = 0.5 is expected to be degenerate.

## A4. MIX(c) needs an explicit convergence rule (medium priority)

MIX(c > 0) is defined from the SO path flows; if SO is nonconverged, MIX is flagged nonconverged even when the follower UE converged (as implemented). MIX(1) equals SO by construction and has no follower solve. **Proposal:** state that MIX(c) is converged only if both the SO reference and the follower UE meet the tolerance, and that MIX(1) and MIX(0) are reported as identity checks rather than independent solves.

## A5. Sensitivity S3 needs its share parameter (low priority, blocking S3 only)

The absolute calibration "total demand as a fixed share of the summed capacity of the links leaving origins" has no value for the share. It is implemented (`reference_demand_absolute(inst, share)`) but omitted from `experiments/EXP-003.json`. **Proposal:** the owner fixes the share before the freeze (for example the share that makes the median total demand equal to the median rho = 2 FF-calibrated demand on exploratory seeds, computed once and frozen).

## A6. Runtime planning values (information)

Projected single-core runtime of the draft full matrix with FW as drafted, from measured means: about 27,000 s (7.5 h), of which EXP-005 about 12,800 s; S1 at `1e-8`, S2 and S3 are proxies, not measurements (see the summary table). The feasibility estimate "under one second per solve" was wrong for congested grids (6x6, rho >= 2: 8 to 12 s per FW solve, mostly at the cap). With GP the full matrix projects to minutes. Peak resident memory per run process was 32 to 35 MiB.

The interval `(80/31, 80/9)` and the regime boundaries `40/11` and `80/9` are now verified by exact rational arithmetic and by the numerical engine (`tests/test_braess_interval.py`). The EXP-002 protocol sentence "to be verified by exact rational unit tests before any run" can be updated to "verified (M2)" at the freeze.

## A8. Freeze record format (information)

`flow run` accepts a confirmatory config only if `research/decision_log.md` contains lines `FREEZE <relative path> sha256:<hex>` for `research/protocol.md` and the experiment's protocol whose hashes match the current files (decision D-007). The owner may prefer another mechanism; the guard is a single function (`flow.runner.freeze_status`).
