Can individually optimal routing worsen collective travel time?

Loading the measured answer.

When every traveller takes the route that is best for them, total travel time can exceed what a coordinated assignment achieves. FLOW is a preregistered simulation study of that gap. This page shows what it measured, on what networks, and how sure it is.
